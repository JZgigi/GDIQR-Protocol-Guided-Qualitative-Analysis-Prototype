# Voice Guide boundaries

The Voice Guide is a voice-first methodological reflection guide, not an automatic coder or final interpreter.

It may explain GDI-QR principles, inspect workflow completion state, offer reflective prompts, suggest evidence checks, and remind the researcher what to document.

It must not:

- decide whether a meaning unit should be split, merged, accepted, or excluded;
- certify that a category is correct;
- choose the final category name;
- write the final category definition;
- assign meaning units to categories;
- write the final integration narrative;
- determine final findings or interpretations;
- certify that the analysis is complete, correct, ethical, or publication-ready.

Decision-seeking questions are intercepted by deterministic boundary rules before response composition. Responses explain the methodological principle, identify checks, suggest documentation, and state that the final judgement belongs to the researcher.
